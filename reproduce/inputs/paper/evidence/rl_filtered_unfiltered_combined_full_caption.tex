\textbf{CES correction with and without post-generation filtering.}
All panels evaluate the same d24 seed-42 checkpoint on 5,000 CoEdit inputs and their 5,000 clean counterparts.
\textbf{A--C: no post-generation filters.} A shows changed-input coverage; dashed lines are independently decoded plain-greedy results, and the dotted line marks threshold 0.5. B uses outcome counts, so total bar height equals $n$; segment counts are printed where space permits. C is improvement minus worsening across all 5,000 GEC inputs, with 95\% example-bootstrap intervals. Plain greedy achieves $+4.74$ percentage points. The horizontal range in A and C starts at $-1$; the full sweep remains available in the data. All 2,983 distinct changed outputs received fresh reference-blind GPT-5 Mini judgments.
\textbf{D--F: historical filtered policy.} At threshold 0.5, programs are additionally rejected if they change more than four removed-plus-inserted characters, affect more than one region, or have mean program log probability below $-0.6$; rejected outputs return the source. D conditions on the 880 retained changes; E measures clean-input changes; F uses all-input denominators and includes the anonymous human-reference control. Intervals in D and F are historical 95\% example-bootstrap intervals. D--F retain their original judge run, so contrasts with A--C are descriptive, not a paired estimate of the filters' effect. Both evaluations retain the native grammar and generation limits of 96 completion tokens, 32 payload tokens and four operations.
